# RCD Corp — LGPD Data Privacy Policy (Internal)

In-universe governance policy for RCD Corp, the fictional company modeled by
`rcd_data`. Written as RCD Corp's own internal privacy policy — the kind a
real Brazilian-headquartered company operating in BR/MX/PT/US would maintain
under Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018). Grounded in the
actual tables the generator produces; see [[ropa]] for the full processing
register this policy points to.

Design-only / narrative document — there is no live subject-rights portal,
DPO inbox, or enforcement tooling in this repo. Sections below flag what's
aspirational vs. what the generator already does today.

---

## 1. Scope

Applies to all personal data RCD Corp processes about:

- **Customers** — `customers`, plus every fact table carrying `customer_id`
  (orders, payments, invoices, `rcd_card_transactions`, tickets,
  `call_center_calls`, `social_dms`, `web_sessions`, `leads`, etc.)
- **Employees & candidates** — `employees`, `attendance`,
  `performance_reviews`, `training_records`, `recruitment_pipeline`,
  `engagement_surveys`
- **Business contacts** — `suppliers` (CNPJ-level, not personal data, but
  named contacts within a supplier org are)

RCD Corp operates in Brazil, Mexico, Portugal, and the United States (see
`company.countries` in `config.yaml`). LGPD is the primary framework because
RCD Corp is headquartered in São Paulo and LGPD applies extraterritorially to
any processing done to offer goods/services to individuals in Brazil.
Portugal operations additionally sit under GDPR; Mexico under the LFPDPPP.
Where those regimes impose a stricter rule than LGPD for their local data
subjects, the stricter rule governs — tracked per-activity in [[ropa]].

## 2. Roles

| Role | Who |
|---|---|
| Controller (*Controlador*) | RCD Corp |
| Operators (*Operadores*) | Cloud/DB hosting providers, email/SMS delivery vendors, payment processor for `rcd_card` transactions |
| Data Protection Officer (*Encarregado*, LGPD Art. 41) | `dpo@rcdcorp.com` (fictional) — single point of contact for ANPD and data subjects |

## 3. Data inventory

The authoritative table-by-table register lives in [[ropa]]. This policy
describes the *rules*; the ROPA describes *what data, why, and for how long*.

## 4. Legal bases in use (LGPD Art. 7)

| Legal basis | Where it's used |
|---|---|
| Contract execution (Art. 7 V) | Orders, payments, invoices, shipments, support tickets, employment contracts |
| Legal/regulatory obligation (Art. 7 II) | Invoices/`transactions` (tax records), payroll (`salary` in `employees`) |
| Legitimate interest (Art. 7 IX) | Fraud/security monitoring (`security_events`), product analytics, `web_sessions` / `shopping_cart_events` |
| Consent (Art. 7 I) | Marketing email/SMS (`email_events`, `campaign_events`), social DMs opt-in, cookie-based `ab_test_exposures` |
| Credit protection (Art. 7 X) | `ltv_tier`, card-transaction risk scoring on `rcd_card_transactions` |

No processing activity in this dataset relies on Art. 7 IV (research) or
Art. 7 VI–VIII (legal claims / life or health protection) — flag it if a new
generator introduces one.

## 5. Data minimization already built into the generator

These are existing design choices in `rcd_data`, not aspirational policy —
worth stating explicitly because they *are* the minimization control:

- `rcd_card_transactions.card_id` is a synthetic `CARD-######` token
  (`generators/finance.py`), never a full PAN — no card-number field exists
  anywhere in the schema.
- `customers.cpf_or_cnpj` and `suppliers` CNPJs are generated by
  `utils/identifiers.py` with valid mod-11 check digits but are not real,
  assignable documents.
- All person-level primary keys are random UUIDs (`utils/identifiers.new_uuid`),
  not sequential or derived from PII.
- **No special-category data** (LGPD Art. 5 II — racial/ethnic origin,
  religious/political/union affiliation, health, sex life, genetic or
  biometric data) is modeled anywhere in the schema. `call_center_calls`
  stores a `recording_url` pointing at voice audio; voice recordings are
  ordinary personal data here (not biometric templates), but any future
  voiceprint/speaker-ID feature would need to be re-classified as sensitive
  and re-registered in the ROPA before shipping.

## 6. Data subject rights (LGPD Art. 18)

RCD Corp honors: confirmation of processing, access, correction,
anonymization/blocking/deletion of excessive or unlawfully processed data,
portability, information on sharing, consent revocation, opposition, and
review of solely-automated decisions.

Process (aspirational — no tooling in this repo implements it):

1. Request received at `privacidade@rcdcorp.com` or via Customer Success.
2. Encarregado logs the request and identifies every table in [[ropa]]
   keyed by the subject's `customer_id` / `employee_id`.
3. Response within **15 days** (LGPD Art. 19 default), extendable once with
   justification.
4. Deletion/anonymization requests cascade through FK-linked fact tables,
   not just `customers`/`employees` — see the FK map in
   `foundation/lakehouse-architecture.md`.

## 7. Cross-border transfers (LGPD Art. 33)

RCD Corp is a single legal entity operating across BR/MX/PT/US, so data
about a customer or employee in one country routinely reaches systems
consolidated in Brazil (and vice versa). Mechanism by destination:

| Corridor | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| BR → PT (and PT → BR) | GDPR adequacy is not mutual with LGPD as of writing; transfers run under RCD Corp's intragroup Standard Contractual Clauses (Model DPA v1) |
| BR → MX | No ANPD adequacy decision for Mexico; same intragroup SCCs |
| BR → US | No adequacy; same intragroup SCCs, plus payment-card data scoped additionally under the card network's own data-residency terms |

Tracked per-activity (which tables actually cross a border, vs. stay in the
country of collection) in the transfer column of [[ropa]].

## 8. Retention

**Open item — not yet implemented.** `rcd_data generate` always replaces
(`if_exists="replace"`); `rcd_data stream` always appends
(`if_exists="append"`) and never expires or purges rows. There is currently
no retention/deletion job anywhere in the codebase. A follow-up
`data-classification-retention.md` should assign a retention period per
table (see the proposed doc list this was drafted alongside) before any
production-shaped deployment relies on this policy for real compliance.

## 9. Security measures

Deferred to a dedicated `security-policy.md` (not yet written). Interim
pointers to what already exists in this repo:

- DB sinks must bind to a specific interface (Tailscale IP), never `0.0.0.0`
  — see `DEPLOY.md` and the CLAUDE.md pitfall on `POSTGRES_BIND`/`SQLSERVER_BIND`.
- `security_events` (in `observability.py`) is the source table for any
  future intrusion/anomaly monitoring.

## 10. Breach notification (LGPD Art. 48)

On confirmed incident involving personal data: notify ANPD within
**3 business days** (per ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 15/2024) and affected
data subjects "in a reasonable time," describing nature of data affected,
technical/security measures used, risks, and remediation taken. Coordinate
with the (aspirational) incident-response runbook once written.

## 11. Owner & review

Owner: Legal + Data & Analytics (see `company.departments` in
`config.yaml`). Review this policy whenever a new generator introduces a
personal-data field, a new country is added to `company.countries`, or a
new sink/processor is introduced.
